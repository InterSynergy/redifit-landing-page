Booking a session without hunting in five different places

Without Redifit

  • You look for trainers in several places at once.
  • You ask about free slots in messages and wait for a reply.
  • You agree on price, place and payment separately with every trainer.

With Redifit

  • You see trainers in one place, as a list and on a map.
  • You check free slots right away, including today.
  • Price, location and accepted payment methods sit next to the trainer's service.

Three steps from idea to booked session

Find, check, book. Every step stays in the app — together with payment and the option to change the date.

Step 1 — Find

Category, location, date

Results as a list or on the map, with a “Train today” section and trainers within 15 km of you.

Step 2 — Check

Qualifications and reviews

Education, courses, specialisations, portfolio and client reviews — before you pay, not after.

Step 3 — Book

Payment and rescheduling

Card, BLIK, cash or bank transfer. Change or cancel the booking wherever you made it.

Events and discounts

Not just one-on-one sessions

Trainers also publish group events and lower prices at set times. You'll find both on the home screen.

  • Events with limited spots — date, times, location, photos, level and price. You see how many spots are left.
  • Happy hours — a discount at hours the trainer sets, on a recurring basis, until a chosen date.
  • Train today — a separate section with slots that are still free today.
  • Favourite trainers — save a trainer and get back to them in one tap.

Events go through moderation before they appear in the app. [NEEDS] moderation rules
