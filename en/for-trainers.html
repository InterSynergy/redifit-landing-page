Clients are looking for trainers. Let them find you

Your profile is the first thing a client sees before booking. Fill it in inside the app.

  • Bio and qualifications — profile name, specialisation, education, experience, completed courses and training.
  • Portfolio — add photos from the camera or gallery and reorder them by dragging.
  • Reviews — reply to client reviews and edit your reply later.
  • Visibility — clients find you in search, on the map and in "Trainers near you".

Service settings are saved on your profile, so start with the bio.

You set services, prices and availability yourself

Service

Name, description, category, duration, gross price and VAT rate.

Place

On site with a map pin, or mobile with a range in kilometres.

Availability

Working hours, breaks, recurrence and a gap between bookings for travel or setup.

Booking limits

How far ahead clients can book, how much notice they need and until when they can reschedule.

Time off

A date range with a note; you take no bookings then. [NEEDS: confirm what time off does]

Happy hours

A discount in chosen hours, recurring, valid until a set date.

Payment methods

You tick what you accept: cash, card, BLIK, bank transfer.

Events

Date, hours, location, photos, level, price and participant limit. Events go through moderation. [NEEDS: moderation rules]

When your calendar has gaps

Four tools you use to drive bookings yourself

All of them are in the app and need no extra advertising.

Happy hours

Lower the price at hours that are hard to fill. The discount runs on a cycle and expires on a day you set.

Group events

One slot, several participants. You set the level, price and spot limit, and add a gallery.

SMS to clients

A message to everyone or to chosen people on your list, up to 200 characters, sent from your phone.

“Book again”

From a client's history, one button takes you back to the same service — for both sides.

Calendar, clients and messages in one place

  • Calendar — month and week views plus the sessions for a chosen day.
  • Booking — client, phone, note, cost and payment method; edit it, add it to a calendar or book again.
  • Clients — a searchable list with each client's booking history.
  • Messages — to all or selected clients (up to 200 characters); sent from the SMS app on your phone.

Payments land in your account and the numbers are at hand

  • Payout account — connect your Stripe account in the app and check its status.
  • Statistics — three tabs: bookings, reports, wallet.
  • Filters — date, client, training type, payment method, ratings, weekdays, hours and discounts.
  • Reports — revenue and sales, downloadable from the app. [NEEDS: report formats]
