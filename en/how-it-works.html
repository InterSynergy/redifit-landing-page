How it works

From search to a booked session

Three steps, all in the app. Below is each one with the screen it happens on, and the rules worth knowing before you pay.

1. Find 2. Check 3. Book Safety

STEP 1

Find a trainer by category, location and date

Pick a training category, type in an address and mark when you want to train. Results show up as a list or on a map.

  • Free slots today — the "Train today" section on the home screen.
  • Trainers near you — up to 15 km from your location, once location is on.
  • Filters and map — narrow results by category, price and time of day (morning, afternoon, evening), and see how many trainers work in an area.
  • Favourite trainers — save a trainer and come back from the home screen.

STEP 2

Before you book, see who you are dealing with

A trainer's profile shows what you need to decide: qualifications, specialisations, portfolio photos and client reviews.

  • Bio and qualifications — education, experience, completed courses and specialisations.
  • Portfolio — photos the trainer added to their profile.
  • Star-rated reviews — sort by newest, oldest, highest or lowest rated, mark a review as helpful or report it. Trainers can reply to reviews.
  • Services with prices — each service shows duration, price and location.

Reviews come from clients after a completed booking. [NEEDS: confirm with backend]

STEP 3

Book and manage the session in one app

Pick a time and payment method, then confirm. The booking lands in the app calendar, where you can change it, cancel it or add it to your phone calendar.

  • Card and BLIK in a secure Stripe form — the app does not store card details.
  • Cash or bank transfer — if the trainer offers them; the trainer sets the payment methods.
  • Booking calendar — tabs for upcoming, completed and cancelled; "Book again" in one tap.
  • The session in your phone — add it to your device calendar or save it as an .ics file.
  • Status notifications — push, email or SMS, your choice in settings.

Change and cancellation rules

Under the trainer's rules, a booking can be cancelled at least 24 hours in advance. A payment already taken may not be refunded. [NEEDS: confirm whether 24 h always applies]

What's looking out for you when you pay in the app

Five mechanisms that work before and after you book a session.

Card details never touch the app

Card and BLIK are handled in Stripe's own form. Redifit doesn't store card numbers.

Phone number confirmed by SMS code

You confirm your number with a code at registration, so accounts can't be opened on a random number.

Reviews only after a session

A rating is left after a completed booking. An inappropriate review can be reported.

Events go through moderation
